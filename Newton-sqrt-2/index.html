Newton's Method for Calculating √2

Newton's method uses the formula: xn+1 = xn - f(xn)/f'(xn)

For √2, we use: f(x) = x² - 2 and f'(x) = 2x

Therefore: xn+1 = xn - (xn² - 2)/(2xn)
