# Pop-It Game

## Overview
Pop-It is a fun, web-based game for kids built using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. This digital version of the popular "Nim" game features a colorful heart-shaped board with bubble buttons that players take turns popping.

- 🎮 **Play Now**: [Vercel Deployment](https://pop-it-theta.vercel.app/)
- 💻 **Source Code**: [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/vuhung16au/MachineLearning-GenAI/tree/main/Pop-It)

## Game Features
- 🎮 **Interactive Gameplay**: Turn-based mechanics on a heart-shaped board with approximately 30 bubble buttons
- 🎨 **Attractive Design**: Colorful visuals with pink and red tones, including animations for popping bubbles
- 🎵 **Audio Effects**: Background music, pop sounds, and game-end sounds
- 🤖 **AI Opponent**: Play against the computer with three different difficulty levels
- 👥 **Multiplayer**: Option for Player vs Player mode
- 📱 **Responsive Design**: Playable across different devices and screen sizes

## How to Play
1. This is a turn-based game where you pop bubbles from the board.
2. On your turn, you must pop 1-3 bubbles (no more, no less).
3. Click "Start Pop" to begin your turn, then click on the bubbles you wish to pop.
4. When done, click "End Pop" to finish your turn.
5. The player who pops the last bubble loses the game.
6. In Player vs AI mode, you can select the difficulty level and who goes first.

## Game Modes
- **Player vs Player**: Play against a friend locally
- **Player vs Computer**: Play against the AI with adjustable difficulty

## AI Difficulty Levels
- **Level 1 (Easy)**: Computer makes random moves
- **Level 2 (Medium)**: Computer makes slightly smarter moves
- **Level 3 (Try Hard)**: Computer plays optimally to win

## Strategy Tip
Try to leave your opponent with 5, 9, 13, or 17 bubbles!

## Setup Instructions
1. Clone this repository

3. No additional installation required!

- Use mouse or touch to interact with the game
- "Start Pop" button begins your turn
- "End Pop" button finishes your turn
