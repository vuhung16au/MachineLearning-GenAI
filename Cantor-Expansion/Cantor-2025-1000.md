# Problem 1: Cantor's expansion of $\frac{1}{2025^{1000}}$ in base 10

Given: 
$$\frac{1}{2025^{1000}} = a_1 + \frac{a_2}{2!} + \frac{a_3}{3!} + \cdots + \frac{a_n}{n!}$$

Here,
1.  $n$ is a **positive integer**.
2.  $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n$ are **nonnegative integers**.
3.  $a_k < k$ for $k = 2, \ldots, n$.
4.  $a_n > 0$.

This is the Cantor's expansion of the fraction $\frac{1}{2025^{1000}}$.
Find n.

# Bài toán 1: Khai triển Cantor của $\frac{1}{2025^{1000}}$ trong cơ số 10

Cho: 
$$\frac{1}{2025^{1000}} = a_1 + \frac{a_2}{2!} + \frac{a_3}{3!} + \cdots + \frac{a_n}{n!}$$

Trong đó,
1.  $n$ là một **số nguyên dương**.
2.  $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n$ là các **số nguyên không âm**.
3.  $a_k < k$ với $k = 2, \ldots, n$.
4.  $a_n > 0$.

Đây là khai triển Cantor của phân số $\frac{1}{2025^{1000}}$.
Tìm n.

## 🔢 Step 1: Prime Factorization of the Denominator ($K$)

The denominator is $K = 2025^{1000}$. We find its prime factorization:
$$2025 = 3^4 \cdot 5^2$$
$$K = (3^4 \cdot 5^2)^{1000} = \mathbf{3^{4000} \cdot 5^{2000}}$$

The condition $\frac{1}{K} = \frac{\text{Numerator}}{n!}$ implies that $n!$ must be divisible by $K$. We must find the smallest $n$ such that:
$$v_3(n!) \ge 4000 \quad \text{and} \quad v_5(n!) \ge 2000$$

---

## 🔍 Step 2: Determining $n$ based on Factors of 5 ($5^{2000}$)

We need to find the smallest $n$ such that $n!$ contains at least **2000 factors of 5**. We use the fact that the exponent of $p$ in $n!$ is approximately $\frac{n}{p-1}$.
$$n \approx 4 \times 2000 = 8000$$

Calculating the exact exponent for $n=8000$ using Legendre's Formula (or direct division):
$$v_5(8000!) = \left\lfloor \frac{8000}{5} \right\rfloor + \left\lfloor \frac{8000}{25} \right\rfloor + \left\lfloor \frac{8000}{125} \right\rfloor + \left\lfloor \frac{8000}{625} \right\rfloor + \left\lfloor \frac{8000}{3125} \right\rfloor$$
$$v_5(8000!) = 1600 + 320 + 64 + 12 + 2 = 1998$$
Since $1998 < 2000$, $n=8000$ is too small.

* $v_5(8005!) = 1998 + 1 = 1999$
* $v_5(8010!) = 1999 + 1 = 2000$

The minimum $n$ for the $5$-factor constraint is $n_5 = \mathbf{8010}$.

---

## 🔍 Step 3: Determining $n$ based on Factors of 3 ($3^{4000}$)

We must check if $n=8010$ satisfies the $3$-factor constraint: $v_3(n!) \ge 4000$.
$$n \approx 2 \times 4000 = 8000$$

Calculating the exact exponent for $n=8010$:
$$v_3(8010!) = \left\lfloor \frac{8010}{3} \right\rfloor + \left\lfloor \frac{8010}{9} \right\rfloor + \left\lfloor \frac{8010}{27} \right\rfloor + \cdots + \left\lfloor \frac{8010}{6561} \right\rfloor$$
$$v_3(8010!) = 2670 + 890 + 296 + 98 + 32 + 10 + 3 + 1 = 3999$$
Since $3999 < 4000$, $n=8010$ is too small. We need 1 more factor of 3.
* $n=8011$ and $n=8012$ do not add factors of 3.
* $n=\mathbf{8013}$ is divisible by 3, adding 1 factor.
    $$v_3(8013!) = 3999 + 1 = \mathbf{4000}$$

The smallest integer satisfying both divisibility constraints is $\mathbf{n = 8013}$.

---

## 4. 📝 Step 4: Verification of the $a_n > 0$ Constraint

The representation is unique for a given $n$, where the final coefficient is $a_n = \frac{n!}{K} \pmod{n}$. If $a_n$ were 0, we could reduce the expansion to $n-1$.

Here, $n=8013$, and $K = 3^{4000} \cdot 5^{2000}$. Let $N = \frac{8013!}{K}$.
$$8013 = 3 \times 2671$$

For $a_n$ to be zero, $N$ must be divisible by $n=8013$. This requires $v_3(N) \ge v_3(8013) = 1$.
$$v_3(N) = v_3(8013!) - v_3(K) = 4000 - 4000 = 0$$

Since $v_3(N) = 0$, $N$ is **not divisible by 3**, and therefore **not divisible by 8013**.
Thus, $a_{8013} = N \pmod{8013} \ne 0$, which means $\mathbf{a_{8013} > 0}$.

The value of $n$ is the smallest integer satisfying the divisibility criteria, which is 8013.

The value of $n$ is $\mathbf{8013}$.
